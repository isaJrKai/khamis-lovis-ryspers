§ 01About

Engineering that earns its place on the ground.

I'm a final-stage Civil Engineering student at Metropolitan International University. My work sits where the classroom meets the construction site: I supervise earthworks, audit reinforcement, test materials, and translate what I learn in the field into publishable research on durable, low-cost construction.

01

On-site, not just on-paper

I log hours on active sites (earthworks, rebar, formwork) so my numbers come from real pours, not just textbooks.

02

Lab-backed claims

Every site observation I publish is paired with a materials test or a measurement. I keep the receipts.

03

Safety first, always

I run HSE checklists as part of the work, not as paperwork. A safe site is a faster site.

04

Cost-aware engineering

I design and review with the budget in mind. The cheapest spec that still meets the code is usually the right one.

The drawing is a promise. The pour is the proof. I work in the gap between the two, where it actually holds.
